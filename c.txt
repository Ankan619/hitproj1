Future enhancement of the system focuses on integrating BERT, a powerful deep-learning language model.

BERT understands sentence meaning, context, and hidden intent far better than traditional ML models.

This allows the system to catch advanced phishing emails, deceptive wording, and context-based spam.

SHAP will also be added to make the model transparent and explain why each email is classified as spam.

This explainability increases trust and helps organizations understand model decisions.

Combining BERT with SHAP improves both accuracy and interpretability.

Existing models like Logistic Regression, SVM, and Random Forest rely mostly on keyword-level patterns.

These traditional models struggle with complex sentences and cleverly disguised spam.

Their performance depends heavily on feature engineering such as TF-IDF.

Meanwhile, BERT learns features automatically, reducing manual effort and improving detection quality.

Compared to classical ML, BERT handles long emails, phishing-style content, and semantic tricks better.

SHAP explanations also provide an advantage over older systems that behave like “black boxes.”

Overall, the enhanced system becomes more robust, context-aware, and future-proof.

With BERT, false positives decrease, so fewer genuine emails get wrongly blocked.

With SHAP, security teams can understand why a prediction was made, which improves decision-making.

This combination makes the spam detection model suitable for enterprise-level deployment.

The project proves that blending traditional ML with modern deep learning gives the best results.

It also shows that explainability is now as important as accuracy in security systems.

The enhanced system provides higher precision, better adaptability, and stronger protection against evolving threats.

In conclusion, the project delivers a reliable, intelligent, and transparent email security solution ready for real-world use.
