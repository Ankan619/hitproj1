Spam Email Detection System Using Machine Learning, BERT, and SHAP aims to accurately identify unwanted and harmful emails.
Email spam, phishing, and scam attacks are increasing rapidly, making traditional keyword-based filters unreliable.
BERT understands the context and meaning of email text, improving the detection of smart and disguised spam.
SHAP provides clear explanations of why the model marks an email as spam, increasing trust and transparency.
The system reduces false positives so genuine emails don’t get mistakenly blocked.
It also catches advanced phishing attempts that normal filters usually miss.
As cyber threats evolve, intelligent spam detection becomes a future necessity.
Organizations, students, and professionals need secure and safe email communication.
The model can be scaled for real-world mail servers and cloud applications.
Overall, the system delivers a smarter, explainable, and future-ready solution for email security.
